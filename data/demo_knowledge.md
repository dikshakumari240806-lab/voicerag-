# Scholarship Program 2026

The National Student Opportunity Scholarship is available to eligible undergraduate students.

## Eligibility
Applicants must be enrolled in an undergraduate program at a recognized institution and must have completed their previous qualifying examination.

## Deadline
The application deadline for the 2026 cycle is 30 September 2026 at 11:59 PM India Standard Time.

## Documents
Applicants should prepare proof of enrollment, previous examination marksheet, identity proof, and bank account details.

## Selection
Applications are evaluated using eligibility checks followed by academic and financial criteria described in the official application guidelines.

## Support
For application issues, applicants should use the support channel listed on the official scholarship portal.
